# Homepage PSW structure render

Source: `assets/structures/variants/PSW_complex.pdb` (user-supplied variant docking archive).
Construct: D281P/G420W/N514S, chain A, 1184 residues.
Rendered from actual coordinates using Mol* cartoon and ligand representations, transparent canvas, rotated 270 degrees to a horizontal composition. PNG has native alpha; no white-key filter is needed.

The car is concept art; the protein is a docking-model render, not an experimentally resolved structure or a simulation of catalytic motion. Coordinate geometry was not modified.
